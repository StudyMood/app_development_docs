## 7. Monetization & Revenue Model
*App paise kaise kamayegi?*

- **Commission:** Restaurant partners se 12% - 18% per order commission.
- **Delivery Fee:** Distance-based dynamic fee (non-subscribers ke liye).
- **Subscription Model (QuickBite Pass):** ₹199/month for unlimited free deliveries.
- **In-App Promotions:** Featured restaurant advertising & sponsored listings.
